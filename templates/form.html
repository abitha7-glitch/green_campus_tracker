What happens when you save

The reading goes into energy_readings. The trigger trg_after_energy_reading_insert then looks up the meter's emission factor and writes consumption × factor into carbon_logs. You never fill in CO₂e yourself.

The app also stops a few things the table constraints can't: a date in the future, a second reading for the same meter on the same day, and readings on a meter marked Inactive.
